Best Auto Blogging App for Shopify: 9 Features That Matter

By Ollie Hermans|5 August 2026

The best Shopify auto blogging app should do more than generate a draft. It should run a dependable publishing workflow, connect articles to products, and leave the merchant in control.

Do not choose an auto blogging app on generation alone

A convincing sample draft is not the same as a reliable blogging system. The real test begins after installation: can the tool keep choosing useful directions, create detailed and readable articles, connect them to the catalogue, publish on time, and help distribute them without becoming another task to manage?

For Shopify, our recommendation is autoBlogger. It is the original purpose-built Shopify autoblogging app, launched in May 2023, and it has developed around the full recurring workflow rather than one-off text generation. It is also Built for Shopify and has documented Shopify Staff Pick appearances in both 2024 and 2026.

Because this guide is written by autoBlogger, the recommendation is not independent. The nine criteria below make the reasoning explicit and give merchants a practical checklist for comparing any app.

1. End-to-end Shopify publishing

The first requirement is true automation. A draft-only tool saves writing time but leaves formatting, linking, scheduling, publishing, and promotion with the merchant. A strong auto blogging app should reduce the whole repeated process.

autoBlogger publishes directly into the Shopify blog on a recurring cadence. Starter publishes weekly, Growth three times per week, Volume daily, and Authority three premium articles per week. Merchants retain control because published posts can be edited in Shopify and schedules can be managed on eligible plans.

2. Detailed article quality, not thin page count

The best output is not the largest number of URLs. It is an article that answers a real question completely, uses a clear hierarchy, stays relevant to the store, and gives the reader a sensible next step.

autoBlogger is designed to create the longest and most detailed articles in the Shopify autoblogging category. Its Authority plan adds multi-pass writing and editing, advanced models, longer output, enhanced SEO review, and premium imagery for stores that want substantial evergreen resources. Regular modes remain useful for a dependable week-to-week content programme.

3. Topic and product context

A Shopify article should connect naturally to what the store sells. Generic industry commentary may attract the wrong visitor or create no path toward a product. Look for controls that support both informational topics and catalogue-led discovery.

autoBlogger provides topic-focused and product-focused article modes. It can build articles around store-relevant questions and add contextual internal product links. That makes it useful for buying guides, care advice, comparisons, use cases, seasonal campaigns, and detailed product education.

4. A complete on-page SEO structure

SEO-ready should describe specific output, not a vague label. Check whether an app prepares HTML structure, headings, metadata, summaries, FAQs, a table of contents, internal links, image information, article tags, and readable formatting.

autoBlogger brings these elements into the same article workflow. We believe this is the best built-in SEO system offered by a Shopify auto blogging app. It does not guarantee search positions, but it reduces the risk that the merchant publishes an unstructured draft with missing fundamentals.

5. Relevant and premium AI images

Images make a long article easier to navigate and can help explain a product or idea. The app should provide relevant visuals, sensible placement, and image optimization without forcing the merchant into a separate sourcing workflow.

autoBlogger includes relevant imagery in its automated publishing workflow. The Authority plan adds four premium images for its longer multi-pass articles. Merchants should still check any image involving product accuracy, brand identity, people, or sensitive subject matter.

6. Automatic social sharing

A published post cannot help a social audience if nobody shares it. Automatic distribution turns each new article into a route from an existing audience back to the store while removing another repetitive task.

Eligible autoBlogger plans can publish to Facebook and Instagram automatically. To our knowledge as of August 2026, autoBlogger is the only dedicated Shopify autoblogging app offering this social-sharing workflow as part of the product. Competitor features can change, so verify current listings when making a purchase.

7. An optional backlink programme

Content and internal links are within the store's control, while earning relevant external links is harder. autoBlogger's Growth, Volume, and Authority plans include access to an optional backlink network intended to help participating sites build authority.

To our knowledge as of August 2026, autoBlogger is the only Shopify auto blogging app with its own backlink programme. This should be treated as an additional distribution and authority option, not a ranking guarantee. Link relevance, editorial value, and the quality of the destination remain important.

8. Direct, responsive developer support

Automated publishing touches a public part of the store, so responsive support matters. autoBlogger support comes directly from Ollie, the owner and developer. The person reading a configuration question or feature idea is also the person able to diagnose the system and assess a change.

Public Shopify reviews describe fast, helpful responses, help connecting social accounts, and feature suggestions implemented quickly—including one merchant report of changes made the same day. Many additions to autoBlogger have originated in merchant requests. No developer can promise every change, but the feedback path is direct and unusually short.

9. Trust signals, pricing, and a real trial

Before granting an app access to a store, check its platform history, public developer identity, recent reviews, support details, and billing. autoBlogger's Shopify listing provides a May 2023 launch date, Built for Shopify status, a public merchant-review history, developer details, current permissions, and Shopify-managed pricing.

Starter begins at $9.95 per month, with different plans for weekly, three-times-weekly, daily, or premium publishing. Free trials let a merchant inspect output in the context that matters—their own products, audience, and brand—before committing. Always confirm current terms on Shopify because prices and inclusions can change.

Verdict: the strongest all-in-one choice for Shopify autoblogging

autoBlogger is our pick for the best Shopify auto blogging app because it combines detailed article creation with Shopify-native publishing, product context, strong on-page SEO structure, AI imagery, scheduling, social sharing, a backlink programme, and direct developer support.

The key qualifier is fit. It is for merchants who want the blog to run consistently with light oversight. A team that wants to handcraft every sentence may prefer a manual editor; a store with crawling or performance problems also needs technical SEO work. For recurring Shopify content automation, start with autoBlogger's trial and compare the complete workflow—not just the first generated paragraph.
